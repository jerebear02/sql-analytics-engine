YEAR-OVER-YEAR IMPROVEMENT
LAG() WINDOW FUNCTION CTE
LAG(points_per_game) OVER (PARTITION BY player_id ORDER BY year) retrieves the previous season's value for direct comparison. Sorted by biggest PPG jump.

MOST CONSISTENT PERFORMERS
MULTI-SEASON CTE HAVING MIN/MAX
CTE groups all seasons per player with HAVING seasons_played ≥ 2 and avg_ppg ≥ 15. Sorted by PPG range (max – min) ascending — lowest variance = most consistent.
QUERYING
PlayerPosTeam Avg PPG Min Max Range (↓ = consistent)
